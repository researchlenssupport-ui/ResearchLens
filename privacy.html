ResearchLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

ResearchLens is a browser extension that provides academic webpage translation, journal information recognition, and academic literature metadata lookup. This policy explains what information the extension may access and how that information is used when you choose to use its features.

Important: Translation and lookup features may send the text or query needed for the feature to the service provider selected or used by the extension. Do not submit confidential, sensitive, or personally identifying material unless you are comfortable with the applicable provider's data practices.

1. Information accessed and processed

  • Webpage content: When you use a translation or page-analysis feature, ResearchLens may read the relevant text on the current supported academic webpage, such as selected text, article titles, abstracts, or other text needed for the feature.
  • Academic lookup information: When you use journal or literature lookup features, ResearchLens may process academic identifiers or metadata, such as a title or DOI, to retrieve related academic information.
  • Extension settings and API credentials: If you configure a third-party translation API, the API credential and related settings are stored in the browser's extension storage on your device.

2. How information is used and shared

  • Translation services: When you request a translation, the relevant text and the information required to authenticate the request may be sent to the configured third-party translation API provider. The provider processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Academic metadata: ResearchLens may send a search query or academic identifier to the OpenAlex API to retrieve academic metadata. OpenAlex's handling of requests is governed by its own policies.
  • Extension developer: This policy does not describe or authorize any separate server-side collection. If a future version sends information to a server operated by the ResearchLens developer, this policy will be updated before that data handling begins.

3. Local storage and API credentials

API credentials and extension settings are stored using the browser extension storage available to ResearchLens. They are not intended to be uploaded to a ResearchLens-operated server. When you use a third-party API feature, the relevant credential may be transmitted to that API provider to authenticate the request. You are responsible for protecting your API credentials and reviewing the provider's terms.

4. Browsing history and tracking

ResearchLens is not designed to maintain a browsing-history log. It does not need a record of the websites you have visited to provide its stated features. The extension may access the current supported webpage when you use its translation or academic-information features.

5. Data retention and deletion

Settings and API credentials stored by the extension remain in browser extension storage until you change or remove them, or uninstall the extension and the browser removes its associated data. Requests sent to third-party services may be retained or processed by those providers according to their respective policies. ResearchLens does not control those providers' retention practices.

6. Third-party services

ResearchLens may communicate with third-party services selected or used for translation and academic metadata retrieval, including the configured translation API provider and OpenAlex. Please review the privacy policies and terms of those services before using them. Their practices are not controlled by the ResearchLens developer.

7. Security

ResearchLens uses the browser's extension storage for locally configured settings and credentials. Network requests should use HTTPS where supported. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so please use care when handling API credentials and sensitive text.

8. Children's privacy

ResearchLens is an academic productivity tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if ResearchLens features or data practices change. The updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised update date.
